This module creates analytic accounting lines for POS Move Entry when
the POS session is closed, based on the analytic distribution models
defined on products.

**Table of contents**

.. contents::
   :local:

Usage
=====

To use this module:

1. Configure analytic distribution models on products or product
   categories.
2. Create a POS session and close it.
3. Analytic lines are automatically generated and group by account in
   the closed POS session.

You can view the generated analytic lines in Accounting > Configuration
> Analytic Accounts > Analytic Lines.
